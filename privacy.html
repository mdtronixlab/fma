Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information FMA - Full Metal Alchemist Fitness ("FMA", "we", "us") collects through thefma.in, and how it's used. FMA is a gym located at 4th floor Above Westside shopping mall, opp. to DSR WOODWINDS, Kaikondrahalli, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560035, India.

Information we collect

Information you provide directly: if you use the "Join Now" form, contact us by phone, email, or WhatsApp, we receive whatever you choose to share — typically your name and phone number — so we can respond to your enquiry.

Information collected automatically: like most websites, we use Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, and Google Ads conversion tracking to understand how visitors use the site and measure ad performance. These tools may set cookies and collect standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited. We don't control what these third-party tools collect beyond configuring them for our own analytics — see their own privacy policies for details: Google Privacy Policy.

WhatsApp

Buttons on this site that open WhatsApp take you to Meta's WhatsApp service outside our website. Any conversation you start there is subject to WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy, not this one.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to membership enquiries, answer questions, and — only if you've contacted us first — follow up about joining FMA. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

This site uses cookies set by Google (Tag Manager, Analytics, Ads) for analytics and advertising measurement. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time; doing so may affect how some site features behave.

Data retention & your choices

We keep enquiry details (like a "Join Now" submission) only as long as needed to respond to you and manage the relationship if you become a member. To ask what information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in how the site works. The "last updated" date above will always reflect the current version.
